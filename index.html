AI Governance Tools

Draft for Council review. These guides approve nothing; the AI Governance Lead confirms every route and tier.

Route FinderAnswer the suite's own screening questions and see only your case's route, by role, with a handover checklist for each stage. Flow BuilderStart from a scenario or any artefact and open the next steps one at a time, with the conditions that decide each one. Role chips show who does each step; highlight a role to see where it is involved. Suite MapAll 52 artefacts and the UC-ID view by lifecycle stage, with the AI Register as the spine, and the links between them. View as a role to see what that role does, decides and contributes to. Legal and Regulatory BasisThe laws, codes, regulator guidance and standards the suite is built on, grouped by theme, each linked to its official source.

Each artefact opens as a PDF from the docs folder (suite pack v3.9.9: README AIG-GOV-01 and Artefact Index AIG-GOV-03 v1.34, Playbook AIG-GOV-02 v19.9.18, Gate Map AIG-DEC-01 v1.13, Agentic Triage AIG-AGT-03 v1.6, Risk Worksheet AIG-ASS-02 v1.10, Deployment and Rollout Plan AIG-OPS-01 v1.12). Workbooks open as a heading summary; the Excel files hold the formulas and dropdowns.

Roles are a pilot design. They come from the draft role table of 29 September 2026, checked against suite v3.9, and are not adopted. Where the suite is silent or two documents disagree, the tools show a proposed pilot position, marked as such, until the Council confirms its own delegations and owners.
