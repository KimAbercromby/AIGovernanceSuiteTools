Council AI Governance Maturity Ladder

A self-assessment across the seven capability domains of the AI Governance Maturity Framework (Playbook §7.6). Your overall maturity is set by your lowest domain, not the average. Draft for review · OFFICIAL · decision-support only.
How to use. Work through it together with the people who run AI governance, ideally once a year (Playbook §7.6.4). It takes about 30 to 45 minutes. For each domain:
  1. choose the level that best describes the council today;
  2. choose the level you are aiming for (your target);
  3. note the evidence behind your answer, such as a document, a record or an audit finding;
  4. agree the action and who owns it, by role.
Weakest-link rule: a council is only as mature as its least-developed domain, so one blind spot sets the overall level. The aim is an honest picture to plan from, not a score (§7.6.4). Use roles, not names.

Maturity levels (Playbook §7.6.2): 1 Initial · 2 Developing · 3 Defined · 4 Managed · 5 Optimised. Domains from Playbook §7.6.3; aligned to ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage). It structures judgement; it does not replace it.
Data and privacy. This tool runs entirely in your web browser. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere; it is cleared when you close or reload the page. Saved assessment files and spreadsheets go only to your own device. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs of visits, as any website host does. Please do not enter personal data about residents or staff; record roles, not names.
