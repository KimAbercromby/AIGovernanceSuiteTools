Council AI Governance Maturity Ladder

A quick self-assessment across six domains. Your overall maturity is set by your lowest domain, not the average. Draft for review · OFFICIAL · decision-support only.
How to use. For each domain, choose the single level that best describes where your council is today. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes with the right people in the room. The result shows your overall band, a profile across the six domains, and the next practical step for each. Weakest-link rule: a council is only as mature as its least-developed domain, so one blind spot sets the overall level.
