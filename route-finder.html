1What's happening?

1aDoes your use match the approval exactly?

Check the AIR-ID, the exact UC-ID, the approved purpose, data, users and conditions in the decision record, and that the Register shows Approved and Active.

2The ten screening questions

These are the Fast-Track Screening questions (AIG-INV-02), word for word. They are factual checks, not a score: one Yes or Unsure means full intake. Don't mark Unsure as No.

3A few more facts for full triage

Action control · answer requiredDoes a person review each individual action before it takes effect? Unsure is treated as No until per-action review is evidenced (Playbook §4.4.6).

Agentic pathway The agency tier sets a minimum pathway (T2 Medium, T3–T4 High, T5 Critical); the higher of it and the risk tier governs. Leave as not yet assessed if Agentic Triage hasn't run.

Procurement · Gate 4 Gate 4 (Procurement) applies wherever a procurement, new contract, licence change or contract variation is needed (choose “New contract or contract change” for any of these). An AI feature under an existing contract or licence, or a free public tool, records Gate 4 as N/A with the reason, but the supplier checks that still apply are completed (Gate Map AIG-DEC-01; proposed, for Council confirmation).

Mandatory escalation triggers (Playbook §4.4.6). Tick any that apply. Each one sets a minimum tier. A confirmed Severe impact (Impact 5) on any impact dimension also sets at least Medium, whatever the likelihood (Playbook §4.4.4).

Risk tierIf the case has already been triaged, what tier did the AI Risk Assessment Worksheet give? Otherwise leave as not yet known: the AI Governance Lead sets the tier with the AI Risk Assessment Worksheet (AIG-ASS-02). A confirmed Severe impact (Impact 5) sets at least Medium. The AI Governance Priority Index (AGPI) Triage Tool (AIG-ASS-01) sets how quickly governance looks at the use, not the route.
