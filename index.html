AI Governance Tools

Draft for Council review. These guides approve nothing; the AI Governance Lead confirms every route and tier.

Route FinderAnswer the suite's own screening questions and see only your case's route, by role, with a handover checklist for each stage. Flow BuilderStart from a scenario or any artefact and open the next steps one at a time, with the conditions that decide each one. Role chips show who does each step; highlight a role to see where it is involved. Suite MapAll 52 artefacts and the UC-ID view by lifecycle stage, with the AI Register as the spine, and the links between them. View as a role to see what that role does, decides and contributes to.

Each artefact opens as a PDF from the docs folder (suite pack v3.5). Workbooks open as a heading summary; the Excel files hold the formulas and dropdowns.

Roles are a pilot design. They come from the draft role table of 29 September 2026 and are not adopted. Where the suite is silent or two documents disagree, the tools show a proposed pilot position, marked as such, until the Council confirms its own delegations and owners.
